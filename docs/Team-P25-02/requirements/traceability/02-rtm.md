## Functional Requirements

Traceability IDs list maintained test cases, historical command/CI cases, or functional regression targets. Test-case definitions are indexed in the [testing records](../../modernization/implementation/testing/README.md); REG-CW targets define expected outcomes and do not establish execution or a pass. REG-CW05-09 remains Partial. “In Progress” means evidence supports only part of the requirement; “Tested” is not final sponsor acceptance. A dash means no applicable case or completed evidence is recorded here. The FR-19 and FR-20 ruleset links document configuration; they do not prove that a controlled failed merge was blocked.
