# Documentation archive

This archive preserves inherited and historical project documentation. Its
contents describe earlier plans, requirements, or application states and may be
outdated or superseded. Use the current [documentation index](../README.md)
for setup, testing, and operational instructions, and consult approved
requirements and implementation records for current project decisions and
evidence.

## Categories

- [Inherited original-team documents](./inherited/original-team) preserve
  materials produced by the previous team.
- [Assessment baseline](./inherited/assessment-baseline) preserves reports about
  the application and development environment as assessed at the time.
- [Modernization plans](./plans) preserve planning documents retained for their design intent; they are historical guidance and do not supersede validated requirements or implementation records.
- [Validated requirements and traceability](./plans/requirements/validation/README.md) preserve the approved baseline, workflows, RTM, and regression scenario catalog.
- `implementation-records/` contains dated records of completed work and its verification evidence.

The [tooling migration](./implementation-records/vite-migration.md), [dependency updates](./implementation-records/dependency-updates.md), [lint and formatting](./implementation-records/eslint-and-formatting.md), and [defect register](./implementation-records/known-bugs.md) preserve their original decisions and dated checks. Current limitations are summarized in the [development guide](../development.md#known-limitations).
